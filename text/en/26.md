# Chapter 26

Nahor was the first of the three shepherds to wake up. He saw his brothers sleeping in a sitting position, their heads leaning to one side. Joseph slept on the floor with his hands under his head. Sophia sat in the middle and smiled at him. She had the child at her breast. He was drinking. She smiled at him in a way that sent goosebumps down his spine. He felt light, and in the right place. He smiled back as best he could. Then suddenly he looked towards the entrance. There stood Rafael, about ten feet tall, with a broad stance and shining gold armour. He looked at Nahor and pointed to a corner of the stable where a feeding trough stood. Nahor looked there. He nodded, jumped up and took the manger to Sophia. He gestured that he would like to put it next to her and Sophia nodded. She stood up with the child and then placed him in the feeding cot with the sheepskin. She nodded to Nahor, "Thank you!"
Nahor: "I will go and get breakfast for us now. We can all use it, I think." He disappeared through the gate.
An hour later he came back. Everyone was awake by now and in joyful anticipation of a nice breakfast.
Amon looked at Nahor, "Where is the breakfast? Didn't you get anything?" He had come in empty handed.
Nahor smiled, "You won't believe this, but ..." He opened the gate fully and a group of people rushed in. Everyone dropped to their knees and put something on the floor: eggs, sausage, bread, milk, fruit, tea ... Amon laughed and Gallum nodded joyfully: "Yes, yes, yes! My brother Nahor can be sent to fetch breakfast." Sophia stood up everyone looked at her. She went to the first kneeler, held out her hand to him and said, "Thank you for your gift and for coming! If you want to see the child, go now to the manger." The man took her hand, looked at her in disbelief, then stood up and carefully went to the manger. Sophia went to the next one and said something else. And so in turn to all who had come in. Each heard something different from her. The first man stood frozen at the manger, marvelling at the child who was not looking at him. But there was a warm glow emanating from him that he just wanted to be in. He was gently pushed away by the next one, went to Joseph and put his hand on his shoulder, "Thank you, brother!" So did everyone who came to the manger.
A young man whom Sophia had just asked to go to the manger held her hand and looked into her eyes, "And what is your name?"
Sophia smiled at him, "Mary. And his name is Joseph."
"Mary and Joseph, the parents of our Lord," he said, stood up and walked to the manger.
The three shepherds put their heads together. Gallum: "An angel told me tonight to tell everyone that the Messiah child is born today, in a stable in Bethlehem."
Nahor: "He told me that too. That's why I told everyone I met on the way."
Amon: "We have to go everywhere and spread the news. Everyone needs to know about it." Everyone nodded. They said goodbye to Sophia and Joseph and left the stable.
