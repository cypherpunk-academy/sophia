# Chapter 15

Sophia entered the rose garden. The hedge around it was thickly overgrown, the rose bushes all withered. No one seemed to have been here for a long time. She put her hands in front of her eyes, fell to her knees and began to cry. Her whole body kept shaking and the tears ran down her cheeks. She bent forward. Her body was shaken again and again. So she lay there and cried and cried until in the end the tears dried up and she had a light, uplifting feeling inside her. She let herself fall exhausted onto her side, then turned onto her back and looked up at the bright blue sky above her. Not a cloud was to be seen.
Suddenly a large, gently smiling angel's head appeared above her. She turned around and straightened up. The angel had taken a step back and was now standing about ten feet away from her. They looked at each other. After a while Sophia asked, "Who are you?"
Angel: "My human name is Rafael."
Sophia laughed, "Rafael. I have heard of you. What is your real name."
Rafael: "You'd forget it in a minute."
Sophia: "I know, but no angel has ever told me that his name is a human name. What is your child's name?"
Rafael: "Sunbeam, sometimes Sun or Sonnie, depending on the child."
Sophia: "If you are Rafael, then you watch over all people living in community and finding themselves."
Rafael nodded.
Sophia: "And now you are there because Anna and Joseph have become afraid. They lost themselves."
Rafael shook his head slightly and smiled gently.
Sophia mused, "Because I ran away from Anna and Josef's fear?"
Rafael put his head to one side. After a short while he shook it again a little.
Sophia: "Hmm."
Rafael: "I don't come to you for the things other people do, but for the things you do to yourself."
Sophia: "Yeah. But the running away ..."
Rafael: "Why did you run away?"
Sophia closed her eyes and put both hands over her heart. She opened her eyes again and looked at Rafael: "I'm scared because of their reaction, I'm scared because of the fear they both usually don't have."
Rafael nodded, "And that's when you lost her."
Sophia closed her eyes again. "Yes," she said after a while. "That's when I lost them. I doubted them."
Rafael waved his hand over Sophia's head, drawing out a greyish-green cloud, which he dispersed in the air with another movement.
Sophia laughed. She suddenly felt light, as if floating. "Why do you give the doubt in me a picture when it disappears from me?"
Rafael: "Because it is beautiful, and you like to see it. But you still have the doubt in you. It has just disappeared from your mind. It's still in your heart and in your stomach. It's going to take some time."
Sophia nodded. --- "Rafael, why did you get so scared?"
Rafael: "Your mother, because she thinks you're dying because you're having a baby without a man. These are her own old fears. They burst open, she can't do anything about it, she didn't understand them then and then they were stored up. And Joseph got scared because he thinks that his family will kill you and your son because he is a claimant to the Davidic throne. Those are his own old fears too."
Sophia thought for a while and then asked, "When can I go back to them?"
Rafael: "When they find out."
Sophia: "What am I going to do then?"
Rafael laughed, "You are chosen. That's what Gebriel told you. It also means that you will be guided. You don't have to worry about what happens next. It just comes when you need to know, and it always comes exactly as it is meant to."
Sophia: "But don't I need to know that now? It's not like I can stay in this rose garden."
Rafael: "When you have taken one step out of the garden, you will know."
Sophia turned towards the entrance and was about to leave.
Rafael: "Wait a minute! Walk through the garden once more, and spread your arms."
Sophia looked at him, "Good!" She spread her arms and at that moment beautiful red rose petals came out from the withered rose branches that were around her. She marvelled and laughed and then walked off, very slowly with her arms outstretched along the main path to the end of the garden. Rose petals appeared everywhere she passed. At the end she turned around and looked at a sea of red roses. Tears came to her eyes. She ran towards Rafael and jumped into his arms. He caught her and immediately put her back on the ground.
Rafael: "Sophia! You can't do that in humanity yet. We are here to look and talk, not to hug."
Sophia laughed, "I couldn't help it." She continued towards the exit and as she took the first step outside, the thought occurred to her, "I'll go to Elisabeth. She knows what I can do now!"

Anna had watched Sophia from the courtyard as she ran towards the abandoned farm and then disappeared into the rose garden. She wanted to give her time, and she wanted to come clean with herself. She did not feel her fear, but something was different, something oppressed her, she felt paralysed and listless. She had not had this feeling for a long time. She sat down on a wooden bench in front of Joseph's house and prayed. Mary sat with her for a while and then went to Klophas and they both decided to go to the farm with the rose garden to look after Sophia and bring her back. After an hour, the two came back and told Anna that they couldn't find Sophia. She was no longer in the rose garden. Anna looked at them in wonder and asked her angel about it, but he remained silent. She decided not to worry and went to the kitchen to make something to eat. Klophas went into the temple cottage where Joseph had disappeared, but soon returned. He shrugged his shoulders as Anna looked at him questioningly.
"Does he want something to eat?" she asked Klophas. He shook his head.
After the meal, Anna told them both that Sophia had probably run home, in any case she was in no danger. Together with Maria, they said goodbye to Klophas and made their way home. At home, Simeon was waiting outside their front door. He was sitting on a log and greeted them with a warm smile.
"Simon!" cried Mary in surprise and ran to meet him. "Simon! You must know where Sophia is."
Simeon: "I think I do."
Anna looked at him in surprise, "She's ... Not here?"
Simeon shook his head, "She's with Elisabeth, I think."
Anna nodded in relief, "Jaah! I could have thought like that." She closed her eyes. Elisabeth was simply the best place to go with a child in her belly. "Then I'll go and make some food and we'll be on our way, won't we?"
Simeon shook his head, "No, we can't. She can only have Elisabeth around her now."
Anna looked at him urgently, "Why?"
Simeon: "I don't know exactly. She's incredibly bright right now and she should be."
Anna: "And for how long?"
Simeon: "As long as it's fine."
Anna: "Are you sure?"
Simeon: "Yes, I'm sure."
Tears came to Anna's eyes. Simeon stood up and took her in his arms. They went into the house.
