# Chapter 27

Simeon leaned on his walking stick and looked up the hill towards the small temple where the son of Sophia and Joseph was to be received into the community of the Jews. The square in front of the temple and the streets around it were full of people. He walked on. When he reached the crowd, he looked around. On the steps in front of the temple, he saw Nahor, who also saw him a short time later. Nahor called out loudly, "Simeon! Simeon! Come here ..." And to the crowd he shouted, "Make way for Simeon!" Gallum heard and ran, pushing, weaving and turning through the crowd, and a few moments later stood before Simeon and held out his hand to him, "Come along!" Simeon followed him as he made his way back through the crowd. Nahor wanted to kneel before him, but Simeon grabbed his arm in a flash, "You do not bend your knees, you shepherds of miracles!" Nahor laughed and from behind Amon tapped him on the shoulder. Simeon turned and they both fell into each other's arms and stayed like that for a long time.
Simeon looked at all three of them, "I am going to do something now that will be a little unfamiliar to you. But it must be done."
The three looked at him in amazement. "What are you going to do?" asked Nahor, "Give me your hands!" He held his out to them and then grabbed the hands of the three. Then he stepped back a little with his right foot and got down on his knees. He laid his head on their hands and prayed a few words just audibly. Then he stood up again and bowed. To the astonished faces of the shepherds he said, "That's it." Amon was about to ask something when someone in the crowd shouted, "They are coming!"
And they came up the path to the temple, Sophia sitting on her donkey with the child in her arms. Joseph walked beside her. The crowd cleared the way for them. Some threw flowers on the road, others shouted "Lord! Lord!" As the three drew nearer, Simeon became more and more restless. His lips trembled a little. Sometimes he cast a shy glance towards them. Gallum came up behind him and put a hand on his back, "Both feet on the ground, shepherd of men, and look straight ahead at the horizon!" Simeon turned and had to laugh, "Gallum, great things are happening here." Gallum looked at him, "When great things happen, you have to have your feet on the ground and a clear view." They looked at each other for a few moments. Then Gallum nodded towards the crowd and Simeon turned around. Joseph was just a few feet away. Beside him was Sophia and in her arms ... He closed his eyes. When he opened them, Sophia was standing on the donkey right in front of him, holding the child out to him. He looked at it, tears streaming down his face, he reached for the tiny little hands and the child seemed to hold them out to him. He fell on his knees: "Lord! My Lord. I thank you and my destiny for allowing me to see you in my lifetime." The child laughed at him and he closed his eyes.
