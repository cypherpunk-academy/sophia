# Chapter 10

Maria took two handfuls of herbs she had picked from the garden and threw them into a jug. Then she took hot water from the cooker and poured it over them. She cut a lemon into thin slices and put it in. Then she took two cups from the shelf, put them on a round wooden tray, added the jug and went with both from the kitchen to her room, which she shared with Sophia.
Sophia was lying in bed with her head turned towards the wall and the duvet pulled over her.
Maria: "There's tea!" She put the tray on a stool that was in front of Sophia's bed and sat down on the floor in front of it. She waited. After a while she put a hand on Sophia's hip. She did not react.
Maria: "HEY!"
Sophia grumbled, but then turned around and straightened up. She was sitting on her bed with her back against the wall, holding onto her blanket. They both looked at each other.
Sophia: "You killed her ..."
"... killed her," Maria added. "You've said that a hundred times now."
Sophia: "You didn't see it! It was so terrible. They were all so blind, so full of fear, none of them were really there, just dark red hatred, dark green cowardice, dark blue lameness, dirty yellow envy, and all totally blind --- except Mother. She stood there and understood everything. She was with herself ... And she does nothing!!!"
Mary put her hands on her heart and closed her eyes.
Sophia continued, "And it all shot to my heart, wanted to enter me, with power! I got such a rage, my voice couldn't scream like I had it in me. I screamed and ran and wanted to throw myself in front of it, but then ..."
Maria: "... mother intercepted you and carried you away."
Sophia: "She's human! She didn't do anything, it's all in their heads, from the priests and the people. There's nothing real about it! She didn't do anything. Thomas did something."
Mary: "It was in her head too. She was with him."
Sophia: "Yeah, maybe something was in her head too."
Maria: "She's still trapped in there. She can't leave."
Sophia bristled and looked at Maria, "Have you spoken to her?"
Maria nodded. "She's here. Right now. Can't you see her? She's sitting on the stool by the window."
Sophia: "No. You know I don't see dead people."
Maria: "I thought because you're so close to her. Make yourself quiet and empty and then look towards the stool. It's not her body, it's just her and her thoughts and feelings. Can't you feel anything?"
Sophia: "No. Nothing." She braced herself again, turned her head to the chair and closed her eyes. Suddenly she said, "No! I didn't abandon you!"
Maria smiled, "See, she's here! That was her thought you were responding to. That's one of them she can't resolve: Why did you stop playing together so much at some point?"
Sophia pondered. "It got boring at some point --- you suddenly became more interested in boys than in the animals. And I didn't understand that. The animals need us. The boys don't need us. You suddenly changed so much."
"And who is Lilith?", Sophia looked towards the stool.
Maria smiled at her, "You don't know who Lilith is? Hmhmhm."
Sophia: "You don't mean the demoness? Lidia?"
She suddenly felt a lot more energy coming from the chair and felt Lidia. She didn't see her, but felt her clearly.
Lidia: "Lilith was just there at some point. And she was so beautiful, so beautiful. And she had an incredible fire in her eyes, and then she moved in such a way that I couldn't look away. It was exciting. And she told me that I have a body with which I can do all that too. Other ... men ... lose their senses when I'm all in my body and happy and let it shine and move like it wants to move, and show my bosom ..."
Sophia: "Lidia!" She looked crossly at the stool. "Lidia, this is a demoness, she wants to get you in her power. That's dangerous."
Maria: "And we need the danger to know ourselves --- you said to me. Not just once."
Sophia: "But not with Lilith. She wants ... she wants women to ..."
Lidia: "She wants women to feel puddly comfortable in their body and not hide it, but try everything with it that it can do."
Sophia: "That scares me."
Lidia: "That's not fear, that's envy. Dirty yellow. I can see that well."
Sophia took a deep breath, "You met Lilith and then you became more interested in boys than animals."
Lidia: "Mmm. Thank you. Now something has flown away from me."
Sophia: "That was it? That's what you were hung up on."
Maria: "That was something, but not everything."
Maria and Sophia looked at the empty stool and closed their eyes. Sophia was startled. She suddenly saw an image of Lidia, she saw her and then again she didn't see her. But there was clearly something there that was Lidia.
Lidia said without moving her mouth: 'Yes, it's something else. People just wanted me gone. They are afraid to stand out from the crowd, and if the one on their left or right gets scared, they get scared too. They didn't care about me at all. I could get away. They just didn't want to get themselves under the wheels. Even the priest. Although he was different, he really believed he had to do this, really believed, with his heart .... Jesus. But the others ... And I didn't want to stand out either. I was so scared to stand there alone, against everyone else, that I couldn't even fight for myself. And then I was alone."
Sophia: "Why didn't you scream when Thomas touched you?"
Lidia: "He promised me.... He tricked me... it was so complicated... we were already so far away from the village..."
Sophia: "How did he trick you?"
Lidia: "He did ... he told me that Alep was waiting for me and wanted to talk to me outside on the rock, alone. He would lead me to him so it wouldn't be so noticeable."
Sophia: "And you were going to Alep, whatever the cost."
Lidia: "Yes. He had kissed me the night before."
Sophia: "Like that?"
Lidia: "Yes."
The three of them were silent for some time.
Sophia: "But then why are you still here?
Lidia: "Because of my mother and because of you. But it's different with you now. I'm just stuck in the fact that you left me. But it wasn't like that."
"And your mother?"
"She won't forgive herself for not telling me I was engaged. And now she wishes me back with all her might so she can tell me. But when I'm with her, beside her, when she's washing or sewing, she doesn't notice. And I can't get rid of her wish, it ties me up. It's like an anchor on the foot."
Maria: "You'll have to wait until she gives it up."
Lidia: "I know. --- And what are you attached to, Sophia? I can see that something is paralysing you too."
Sophia: "I don't want to go out into this adult world. It's so full of fear of doing something wrong. The law is so strong and soon comes the punishment instead of putting understanding before it. I can't punish or be punished without understanding. I can't get through how to do that, that closes all hearts. There's no life there anymore, that's just doing what's required of you and at the same time secretly seeing how you can have it comfortable and easy for yourself. No risk, no going out, not when there's really something at stake. Otherwise everyone is nice to each other, helps each other, but when it's really about something, everyone freezes. And I can't live in that. I don't want to live in that."
Lidia: "Now something has come loose in you too. I can see that quite well. --- I'll go and see my mother. Maybe I'll have some luck."
Sophia looked at Maria, "She's gone."
Maria nodded, "And is it better?"
Sophia put her hand on her heart, "Yes, it is better. I think I can move a little more again. Have you fed the animals?"
Maria smiled and nodded, "Sure."
Sophia: "Then I want some tea now."
Maria smiled, "I'd love some." She poured a cup and handed it to her.
Sophia clasped it with both hands and took a sip. Then she closed her eyes, "But it hasn't gone away. I know more now why I'm stuck, but people ..."
Maria shrugged her shoulders and looked at Sophia, "... are the way they are."
"But how am I supposed to find my way through that? I mean, there everyone was so ... so blind ... so hard as stone ... and so closed in on themselves."
"They're not always like that. Only when they're afraid."
"They are always afraid, sometimes more and sometimes less."
"Mother isn't afraid.
"Sometimes."
"Rarely." Maria pinned herself, put her arms back and hunched slightly so that her breasts showed slightly in her shirt. She laughed, "Or with love ..."
Sophia looked at her grimly, "Maria! You're 12!" but then had to laugh. "Have you also ... talked to ... Lilith?"
"Sure! After what Lidia told me, right away."
Sophia looked Maria in the eye and laughed.
