# Chapter 25

Joseph and Sophia slept soundly between the ox and the donkey. Suddenly it became a little brighter and warmer in the stable. Sophia woke up and sat down. She supported herself with both arms behind her back and nudged Joseph with one knee. He grumbled, turned and straightened up too. He looked at Sophia: "You look better. Fresher --- but what is it?"
Sophia: "The child, it's coming."
Joseph: "You are not in pain at all?"
Sophia smiled, "No. None." Then she lay down on her back and breathed. Suddenly her whole body felt warm and free, she relaxed her belly and closed her eyes.
Throughout the birth, Sophia felt no pain. Joseph accepted the child. He took it in his arms and it made a short gurgling sound. Then it opened its eyes briefly and immediately closed them again. Joseph looked at the child, he felt drawn to it. A cloud of warmth wafted through him. He had to laugh out loud because of it. His whole body felt full of vitality. He looked at it again and couldn't believe how different life felt with it. He gave it to Sophia on her stomach.
Sophia carefully took the child in her hands. She laughed too. The child did not make a face. She put it to her breast, "Well, do you want to drink?" The child made a few sucking movements on Sophia's breast, but let it go after a short while. Sophia's body had become too cold. No milk came. She tried again to put the child to the breast ... and again, but it didn't help. After a while she told Josef to go to the innkeeper and ask for a little milk.
Josef knocked on the door of the main house. The innkeeper appeared after some time in his dressing gown. His eyes were almost closed: "What is it now?"
Josef: "It was wonderful of you how you rescued us from this bad situation earlier."
Host: "What do you want? It's the middle of the night."
Joseph: "My wife, Mary, has just given birth. She has grown too cold. The milk is not coming. Can we perhaps warm ourselves at your place? Or do you have some milk for us?"
Host angrily: "It's the middle of the night. The house is full and I have no milk for you. I really should have planned this better."
The innkeeper tried to close the door, but Josef held it open.
The innkeeper scowled at him, "Get out of here now! I may as well throw you out of the stable again."
Josef was frightened and withdrew his hand. The innkeeper closed the door.
Back at Sophia's, he was about to say, hanging his head, that he had not received anything, when he saw the child. It was trembling all over. Sophia tried to warm it, but her own body was still too cold. Joseph knelt beside it, pulled up his shirt and carefully lay on the child. It was still shivering. He looked at Sophia in despair. She looked at him and closed her eyes.

At that moment, the barn door opened and Gallum's head appeared. Joseph and Sophia straightened up and looked at him. Gallum turned and said stammeringly to the other two who were behind him, "They're here!" Then he cautiously walked in. Joseph and Sophia marvelled at him as he pulled a beautiful sheepskin from under his shirt and held it out to them both. He gave it to Joseph and fell to his knees, tears running down his cheeks, he folded his hands and said towards the child, "My Lord! Saviour of Israel, the Messiah child of us all, I bring you here a small gift. Please take it from your servant." Sophia marvelled at him with her mouth open. Joseph wrapped the child in the warm, soft sheepskin. It stopped trembling and smiled briefly, almost invisibly. Sophia was still stunned by the way Gallum had spoken, and that he had a warm coat with him, and that he was here at all.
Gallum rested his forehead on the ground, feeling a warm cloud pass through his body, and laughed out loud inside. Amon came up beside him on his knees and pushed him aside. He held out the milk jug to Sophia. "Mother of our Messiah, who will heal the world and King of Israel for all time, please take this small gift from the servant of your Son." She took the jar, still amazed, dipped a finger into it and put it in the child's mouth. He asked for more and carefully she held the jar to his mouth. Tears ran down her cheeks and she nodded briefly in thanks towards Amon. Amon fished for a straw on the floor, shortened it and put it into the jug and the child's mouth, who immediately began to suck on it. Then he put his forehead on the ground too.
Joseph put a hand on both of their heads and said, "Come up, friends! Sit down even with us. Let us pray together."
Nahor joined them and gave Joseph the money, "You will need this. Take it."
Joseph put a hand on his shoulder, "Thank you, brother, it comes just at the right moment. I spent my last money at an inn this morning."
They sat in a circle around Sophia and the child. Held their hands and began to pray. Three great angels entered the room and there was light. They stood around the circle and clasped their hands. Sophia looked up, saw the angels and thought, "Michael --- Gabriel --- Rafael". Then she looked at the child who seemed to be smiling. At that moment, the host of angels who had led the shepherds here appeared above them. Not that they would have had room in such a small stable, but still they were all there and visible and not crowded. Sophia wondered, shook her head and looked around in wonder in all directions. She pressed the child to her heart and felt warm, she saw the three shepherds and Joseph sitting around her praying with their eyes closed. And behind them Michael, Gabriel and Rafael and countless other angels she did not know. She closed her eyes and said, "I thank you with all my heart!"
