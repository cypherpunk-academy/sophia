# Chapter 8

The sun was just setting in Cana. It was seen from Zechariah and Elizabeth's house, disappearing from the bedroom window behind the houses. The two of them did not see it. They were lying in bed together, tightly embraced. He lay on top of her and gently grasped the back of her head. She breathed loudly and said "I love you." He smiled. Then suddenly he embraced her more forcefully, pulled her to him and held her. He groaned loudly. A few moments later they were lying next to each other in bed, both beaming with joy. Elisabeth held her lower belly and closed her eyes. Energy flashed through her. She breathed in and out slowly. Another burst of energy. She looked at him out of the corner of her eye and began, "Mhmh, I didn't think we'd have so much ..." She faltered. He looked at her in amazement. She looked into his eyes and reached for his hand. Her mouth opened in amazement, she looked up at the ceiling and began to breathe deeply and her face glowed even more. Zacharias clutched his chest and began to laugh. She made him pause. And he looked at her.
After a few moments she said, "He's here! He is there!" Tears ran down her eyes. "He has arrived." She held her stomach and looked down. "He gave me a hug. He is so happy to be here. He called me --- mother."
Zacharias hugged her and tears ran down his cheeks.
"He told me he's grateful I waited this long and never doubted. He says it is now from that alone that he has so much faith in me after 45 years. And he needs that for his job." Zacharias marvelled. A thousand thoughts flashed through his mind. One of them was, "Good thing I can't talk. I would ask so much nonsense now..." He folded his hands in prayer and Elizabeth did the same. Then they hugged again, for a long time. Elisabeth kept laughing and looking up. Then she said, "We will need a bigger bed." Zechariah smiled at her: "I'll go to Joseph tomorrow and order one."
