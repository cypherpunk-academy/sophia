# Chapter 20

Sophia sat on the bench in front of the temple house, wrapped in a sheepskin and a blanket. Her belly was clearly visible underneath. She held it with both hands, looked at it and laughed loudly. Joseph came and sat beside her, put a hand on her belly and smiled at her. She smiled back with an unreal warm and tender smile. Josef saw through it every time she did. Behind her stood a larger than life angel with a powerful stature, fanciful mitlitary armour and an elaborate, powerful sword at his side. Sophia looked at Joseph, "Do you see him now?"
Joseph shook his head, "No, but I believe you, I feel his power, he is like Michael I think, just as you say."
Sophia: "What's going to happen to me while he's around? And I still have my guardian angel.
Joseph: "We have to be careful. The priest was in Jerusalem again, I heard. He managed to get free of the accusations. That doesn't bode well."
Sophia: "He can't hurt us. I told you." She smiled again. "And I can see angels now, like Mary. Ever since that night in March ..."
Joseph shuddered, "Don't remind me. After that night, I rejected you."
Sophia: "And then you took me to you." She kissed him on the cheek.
"Joseph!" called Klophas over from the carpenter's shop. "Joseph, someone is coming!"
Joseph jumped up, "Sophia, go to the temple cottage. Just to be sure."
He ran towards the path leading up to the courtyard and recognised an elderly man in a priest's robe coming up the path to the courtyard with quiet steps. He narrowed his eyes, "Simeon?" he thought. And sure enough, when he was close enough and took off his hood, Joseph recognised him. He ran towards him and they fell into each other's arms. After looking at each other for a long time, Joseph said, "Welcome! My brother."
Simeon: "Thank you. I hope all is well here."
Joseph: "Yes, all is very well here. Sophia is in the best of spirits as always. Nothing can shake her."
Simeon: "That will come ..."
Joseph: "I can't imagine that."
Simeon: "It's already beginning. There is news from Jerusalem. But let's go in. I have to tell everyone."
In the temple cottage, all four sat on the floor on cushions that Sophia had embroidered over the last few weeks. They all looked at Simeon. He made a serious face.
Simeon: "All Jerusalem is already talking about it. And soon a crier will come here to Nazareth and announce it. Rome needs money for wars, and soldiers. And that's why the emperor has taken the idea of counting all the inhabitants of the territories occupied by Rome. To check this, they then compare it with old family lists. That's why, and this is the difficult part, everyone has to go to where their family has its tribe in order to register and pay the taxes. And where there are discrepancies, they send investigators."
Joseph: "I can be dead. My family doesn't know that. I'm missing from the list and they can't tell where I am."
Simeon: "The Romans know where you are. They're tracking all kinds of heirs to the throne."
Joseph: "Yes, and then they look at the circle of people around me, how many I can command, and then they find Sophia and Clophas. That's not interesting to them. I'm not a threat to them."
Sophia looked at him, "Hey! You can't command me. We are not married."
Klophas: "You can command me. I like to obey you. It often pays off." He laughed. Joseph stroked Klophas' head with his hand.
Simeon: "But Romans obey laws and orders of their emperor. They like to do that. And when he says let everyone be counted and pay their money, the Romans in Jerusalem try to get as many and as much together as possible."
Joseph closed his eyes and looked at the ground.
Sophia: "This is going to be complicated."
Clophas: "I'll look after Sophia when Joseph is in Bethlehem."
Simeon: "You have no family, there is no head of the family. You can't stay here alone. The priest would take advantage of that right away. He is clever. And he'll try anything to get you."
Joseph: "I'll stay here. We have strong angels here to help us. It's a risk, but all of life is a risk."
Sophia: "The angel nods."
Klophas: "What is his name?"
Sophia: "He hasn't told me yet. He hasn't spoken at all yet."
Klophas looked at her in wonder, "And where is he now?"
Sophia: "Here. Next to me." She tapped the floor with one hand.
Klophas reached there with his hand, trying to feel something.
Sophia: "He's smiling at you."
Klophas tried to smile back but didn't really know where.
Joseph stood up and walked around for a while. Then he turned to the others and said firmly, "We're staying here!"
