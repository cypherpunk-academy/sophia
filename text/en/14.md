# Chapter 14

Thunder rumbled all the way into the temple room. A wind came up, got stronger, howled around the little house and shook the wooden shingles on the roof. Then thunder again and an occasional flash of lightning. Sophia and Josef slept soundly. There was no sign of movement on their part.
Suddenly there was a tremendous clap of thunder and almost simultaneously the room lit up brightly. Sophia and Josef turned a little in their sleep, but did not wake up. At their feet stood a larger-than-life angel with a powerful stature, broad-legged and a great sword at his side. He wore a suit of armour made of all-silver with a golden breastplate and golden stripes, and calmly looked first at the whole room and the altar, and then at the two sleepers. He looked at them with a warm, benevolent gaze. Then he drew his sword, held it up, placed it on the floor in front of him, went down on one knee and bowed his head.
Two doors opened on either side of the altar, where before there had been only a wall, and out came angels, one after the other, in quiet step, forming a circle around Joseph and Sophia from two sides. Each carried a red burning candle in front of them. They all had a slightly different rhythm to their gait, wore a wide variety of clothes, none of them was clearly male or female, but in a strange way it all fitted together, it was a harmony, albeit a very agitated one. When the circle was complete, all the angels placed their candle on the floor in front of them and put their arms on each other's shoulders. They also went down with one knee on the floor and bowed their heads. The great angel got up, went to Joseph's side, knelt down, put a hand on his chest and said, "Your will be done, you prepare a way for the Lord." Joseph turned to Sophia in his sleep so that he was lying right beside her, one hand gently grasping her shoulder. He was still fast asleep. The angel got up and went to Sophia's side, knelt down and put his forehead on the ground in front of her. After a while he got up, went back to his place, knelt there and again put his forehead on the ground. All the other angels followed suit.
A tremendous flash of light in absolute silence lit up the whole room. Through the glare of the light, it was briefly possible to see Joseph lying completely on top of Sophia and embracing her.
Sophia opened her eyes and looked around. It was bright outside, the sun was shining. She looked beside her and saw Joseph sleeping facing away from her. She straightened up and was startled. A large angel was standing in front of her and smiling at her.
Sophia: "Gabriel! --- You have frightened me. What is here? Why is it already light? Did we ... slept here?"
Gabriel: "Sophia! Chosen by the Lord God, you healer, called out of the circle of humanity and entrusted with a great task." Sophia was startled and looked down. She wondered at this form of address. Gabriel had not spoken to her like that the last time. He continued, "Fear not! I bring you great news, from the community of the heavens, from the Lord God! You will bear a son, he will become great and rule over the world, he will be called the Son of the Most High. You will call him Jesus. He is of the house of David and he will rule over the tribe of Jacob in all the ages to come. He will free the world from the burden of lies, untruth and untrust. He will free you from the chains with which you humans bind yourselves to your past and your future, from which you cannot free yourselves. He will bring peace where there is opposition and war. He will heal what needs healing."
Sophia grabbed her stomach with both hands and looked at him for a long time. Then she looked at Gabriel, "But how is that possible, I haven't been with a man yet?"
Gabriel: "The healing Spirit has overshadowed you, the Most High, God, the Lord has wielded his power over you. Look at your aunt Elizabeth, she is six months pregnant, at an old age, and all her life before that she was barren. Nothing is impossible for the Lord God. He will make you the mother of His Son." Gabriel was gone.
Sophia folded her arms in front of her chest and sank her head to the floor.
Joseph awoke, opened his eyes and straightened up. He saw Sophia lying huddled in front of him and wanted to put his hand on her back, but he could not. A force prevented him.
Joseph: "Sophia. What's wrong with you?"
Sophia straightened up, turned and looked at him.
Joseph widened his eyes in amazement and grabbed his chest with both hands, "Sophia! How ... how is this possible? --- You have grown up! You have become a woman. What happened?"
Sophia said nothing, she smiled softly at him, her eyes shone and she held her belly.
Joseph looked at her belly, his face turned pale, his eyes dark, his whole body began to tremble.
Sophia: "We are going to have a son."
Joseph remained silent, fighting the fear that spread through his body --- then everything inside him went black.
Sophia looked at him in horror. She cried out, "What is the matter with you? Why ... Why the fear?" She got up, opened the door of the temple cottage and ran out into the courtyard.
The sun hit her with full force, spring sun. She stopped, closed her eyes and spread her arms. She seemed to inhale the rays, through every pore of her body. Her breathing quietened and suddenly she was in the scene that had taken place in her room the night before while she slept. She saw Michael put down the sword, saw the circle of angels with the candles, the flash of light in the silence and Joseph above her ... She laughed and put a hand on her stomach.
"SOPHIA!" she heard a voice beside her, "SOPHIA! There you are. I was so worried."
Sophia turned to her mother, who had hurried Maria and Klophas away up the driveway to the courtyard.
"Mother!" she said joyfully.
Anna looked at her with wide eyes and grabbed her hands, "Oh! By God, what happened?" She looked at them and could not believe what she was seeing.
Sophia: "The power of the Most High has overshadowed me. I will give birth to a son and he will be called the Son of the Most High, the Son of God!"
Anna shook her head, she knelt down and pulled Sophia down with her: "We must pray. We throw ourselves into the arms of our angels and ask them to draw the shadow from us." Sophia carefully disengaged her hands from Anna's and stood up. She placed her hand on Anna's head and said, "The Most High has made me what I am. I am his servant. I do what He asks of me."
Anna looked at her wildly. Like Joseph, she turned pale in the face and black in the eyes. She could not speak.
Sophia took a step back, looked around, saw Joseph coming out of the temple cottage. He was still trembling. She looked at her mother and then ran around her down the driveway to the farm, past Mary and Clophas, and then across the fields to the other side of the valley where the abandoned farm with the rose garden stood.
